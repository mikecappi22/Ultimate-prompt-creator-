Ultimate Prompt Creator Mobile

Connect your iPhone securely to Ollama running on your Windows PC, then use the Fast Creative Director and Fast Vision Lab from mobile.

V11.7 MOBILE

1. Choose mobile connection

🔒 Secure HTTPS — Recommended
Use Tailscale Serve. Your Ollama endpoint stays private inside your Tailscale network and your iPhone gets an HTTPS URL that Safari can call from the GitHub-hosted app.
🏠 Same-Wi-Fi LAN — Advanced
Use http://192.168.x.x:11434. This may be blocked by iOS/Safari when the Prompt Creator itself is loaded over HTTPS, so use this mainly for testing.

2. PC secure setup

On your Windows PC, install Tailscale and sign in. Install the Tailscale app on your iPhone and sign into the same account.
1
Keep Ollama private on the PC
[Environment]::SetEnvironmentVariable("OLLAMA_HOST","127.0.0.1:11434","User")
Then fully quit Ollama and reopen it.
2
Expose Ollama only to your Tailscale network
tailscale serve --bg 11434
3
Find your private HTTPS address
tailscale serve status
Copy the HTTPS address it shows, such as https://my-pc.example.ts.net.

3. Save your Ollama endpoint
